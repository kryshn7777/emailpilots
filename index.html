Inbox placement & legal footer

Sending style & legal footer advanced · default: Personal

Personal (default) — reads like a normal 1:1 email, no footer. Best for personal outreach; helps land in the main Primary inbox.

Bulk — adds an unsubscribe link + your mailing address (legally required for mass mail). Use only for high-volume sending; the extra footer & markup can push mail into Gmail's Promotions tab.

Getting this wrong has real consequences: Bulk on a few personal emails → lands in Promotions; mass mail without Bulk's unsubscribe footer → can breach anti-spam law.

Personal strips heavy styling and hides the footer so email lands in Gmail's Primary tab. The unsubscribe link and mailing address below are only included in Bulk mode — choose Bulk for high-volume sending where an unsubscribe link is required.

Required by anti-spam law for bulk email. Appended to the footer in Bulk mode.

Everything stays on this PC. Your email password and sign-in tokens are encrypted using Windows’ built-in protection. The AI runs entirely on your computer — no cloud, no account, nothing sent anywhere.

How your data is stored
  • Passwords and sign-in tokens are encrypted, and are tied to this Windows account on this PC.
  • Contacts, replies and drafts are kept in a folder on this PC and are not encrypted — anything running under your Windows account can read them.
  • File names show which of your own accounts sent mail, even though the contents are protected.
  • Nothing is uploaded, and the AI never makes a network connection. The only download is the model file itself.
