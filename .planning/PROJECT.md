# Email Pilots — "The Overnight Flight" Website

## What This Is

An award-tier 3D scrollytelling marketing website for Email Pilots (the Windows desktop app for personal outreach and follow-up email). A camera attached to a paper plane flies one unbroken shot through a volumetric-cloud world — the story of an email getting delivered overnight — with every feature in `New Website/FEATURES.md` woven into the narrative and a complete flight-manual features page. Lives in `New Website/`, fully separate from the legacy `landing/` site.

## Core Value

The visitor's first 10 seconds produce a "this runs in a browser?" reaction that makes them scroll to the end and share the link — and by the landing they know exactly what Email Pilots does and download the trial.

## Constraints

- **Positioning**: never "cold email"/"mass email"/etc. (payment-processor risk) — grep-enforced
- **Tech stack (user-mandated)**: Astro 5 + React islands + TS strict + vanilla Three.js + GLSL + postprocessing + GSAP ScrollTrigger + Lenis + Swup; vanilla CSS
- **Assets**: procedural-only + one committed CC0 HDRI; GLBs generated by dev-only /bake route
- **Performance**: volumetrics own the frame budget; HIGH/MED/LOW tiers mandatory
- **Accessibility/SEO**: all story copy is real DOM; reduced-motion = readable article; Lighthouse SEO/A11y ≥ 95
