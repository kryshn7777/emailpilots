<objective>
Chapter 2: the handoff. The letter folds twice, slides into an envelope, and two more envelopes stack behind it wearing printed wait tags. The sun finishes leaving, the lamp clicks on, and the desk is armed for the night.

This is where the product's core object grammar is taught without a word of explanation: a sequence is a stack of ready envelopes with waits. Everything Ch3, Ch4 and Ch5 do depends on the visitor having learned it here.

It is also the chapter that proves the register strategy. The CC-BY envelope meshes arrived from ingest as bare geometry; here they get the project's paper family and have to look like they were scanned in the same session as the binder sitting beside them.

Purpose: EXP-03's second set-piece, EXP-02's first anticipation-payoff pairing (flap closes as the headline lands), EXP-06's first in-world type.
Output: the reusable envelope set plus a complete Ch2, measured and captured.
</objective>
