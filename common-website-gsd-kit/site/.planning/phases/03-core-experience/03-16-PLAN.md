<objective>
Chapter 5: she answered.

A near-dark room. Nothing moves for fifteen percent of the chapter — the longest silence on the site. Then one envelope drifts in through the window, against every direction the visitor has been trained to expect, and lands in the lamp pool. Two words appear. A waiting follow-up lies down flat, its tag fades, and it slides back under the clipboard. Dawn floods the room. The lamp clicks off.

This is the shareable heart and the product's whole argument in fifteen silent seconds: the machine stands down the moment a human answers. It is also the one chapter where a dropped frame is a ship-blocker, because a hitch in the middle of that silence is the difference between a beat that lands and a beat that reads as a bug.

Purpose: EXP-02's second signature transition — the payoff the previous two chapters set up.
Output: Ch5 complete, verified with a sustained frame-time check rather than spot samples, and captured.
</objective>
