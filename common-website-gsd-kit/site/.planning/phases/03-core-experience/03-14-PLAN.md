<objective>
Chapter 3: the desk sends by itself. Three envelopes lift, pause, glide out through the window, and become points of light with times attached — 9:14, 9:41, 10:07. Never at nine o'clock sharp.

This is the site's first "wait, what?" beat, and its power comes entirely from restraint: ten percent of silence before anything moves, a camera that watches rather than follows, and three departures whose arcs are visibly different from one another. The visitor learns that the desk behaves like a careful person, not a cannon — which is the whole positioning argument made without a sentence about it.

Purpose: EXP-02's trained departure grammar (which Ch5 will reverse), EXP-03's third set-piece, EXP-06's first billboarded world type.
Output: the night HDRI ingested and a complete Ch3, measured and captured.
</objective>
