<objective>
Turn the Phase-2 photo-essay shell into the pinned one-take stage, and give the visitor the two DOM affordances the storyboard promises: the quiet nav from Ch3 and the always-on progress rule.

The pinning mechanism is deliberately boring: CSS `position: sticky` under `html[data-mode='full']`. Lenis animates the browser's real scroll position, so sticky just works, `Timeline.measure()`'s `getBoundingClientRect` math needs no change, and no pin-spacer is inserted between the sections it measures. The cost is one discipline item — `data-mode` must be on `<html>` before first paint, or the restyle lands as a CLS charge.

Purpose: EXP-01 (continuous scrubbed journey on native scroll), EXP-03 (the 740vh map), EXP-06 (choreographed typography), EXP-07 (progress affordance) all get their shell-side machinery in wave 1.
Output: a sticky FULL-mode stage, one additive Timeline extension, a pure affordance module with tests, and the SplitText helper chapters will call.
</objective>
