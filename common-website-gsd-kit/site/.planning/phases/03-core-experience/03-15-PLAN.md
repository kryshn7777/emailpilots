<objective>
Chapter 4: six days over one desk. The room becomes a sundial — light wheels around, shadows sweep the floor like clock hands, envelopes lift into the dark at scattered minutes and a constellation of timestamps accumulates above the desk. A paper day-tag flips MON to SAT. Then one envelope comes back the wrong way, takes a red mark, and files itself into a drawer.

This is the shareable one — the fifteen seconds whose caption writes itself. It is also, by the storyboard's own label, **the budget chapter**: everything the site can do is running at once, and the numbers are checked before the polish, not after. If ≤75 draw calls and ≤5 ms JS do not hold here, the scene gets redirected using the storyboard's authored degradation order rather than being tuned by hope.

Purpose: EXP-02's first signature transition, EXP-03's most demanding set-piece, D3-15's first and most important budget gate.
Output: the remaining two skies ingested, WOW A built, its numbers recorded, and the register formally reviewed under the wheeling light.
