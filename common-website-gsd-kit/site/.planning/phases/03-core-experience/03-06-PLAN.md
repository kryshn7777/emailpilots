Build the object-motion half of the D3-01 vocabulary: the reused envelope flight grammar, the letter fold rig, and the lamp's light-state accent.

Purpose: EXP-02's anticipation → payoff beats have a shared, testable definition before any chapter uses them.
Output: two pure modules with unit tests plus two thin three.js wrappers that bake in the demand-render discipline.
</objective>
