<objective>
Bring the envelopes into the world — the object the whole story is told with — and do it without letting their icon-grade source textures anywhere near the frame.

ART-DIRECTION's "Real Under the Lamp" strategy names this exactly: icon-grade meshes are re-surfaced UP, never displayed as downloaded. The pipeline half of that is stripping textures at ingest so the only surface the renderer can possibly use is the project's own `Paper001`/`Cardboard004` family. It is also what makes the ledger's geometry-class ceilings (0.3 MB and 0.05 MB) achievable at all — the source textures alone would blow them.

These are also the project's only two CC-BY obligations. The attribution text enters the ledger here, at download time, while the credit is on screen — not reconstructed from memory in Phase 5.

Purpose: D3-08's just-in-time ingest and D3-09's register strategy, executed on the pipeline side.
Output: one small `ingest.mjs` extension with a fixture test, three shipped rows, two filled attribution cells.
</objective>
